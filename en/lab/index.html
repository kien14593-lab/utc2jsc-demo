LAS-XD 1398 Laboratory

Nationally accredited construction testing laboratory

LAS-XD 1398 is UTC2 JSC's construction testing laboratory, accredited under Vietnam's national LAS-XD scheme. The laboratory serves major transport and civil projects across southern Vietnam with accurate, timely and legally valid test results.

Testing capabilities

  • Soil & aggregates: physical–mechanical properties, compaction, CBR, gradation
  • Cement & concrete: mix design, compressive strength, elastic modulus, durability
  • Asphalt & bituminous mixtures: Marshall, extraction, penetration, softening point
  • Steel & welds: tensile, bending and weld quality testing
  • Field testing: SPT, plate load test, coring, rebound hammer, ultrasonic testing
  • Structural inspection: load testing of bridges, deflection measurement, safety assessment

Quality assurance

  • Procedures compliant with current TCVN standards and technical regulations
  • Periodically calibrated equipment with full traceability
  • Trained, certified technicians; supervised by university experts
