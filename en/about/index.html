About UTC2 JSC

Who we are

UTC2 Joint Stock Company (UTC2 JSC) is a consulting, survey, design, testing and inspection company operating within the ecosystem of the University of Transport and Communications – Campus in Ho Chi Minh City (UTC2). The company brings together lecturers, PhDs and engineers of the university, combining academic research with hands-on engineering practice on real projects.

Fields of activity

  • Survey and design of transport, civil and technical infrastructure works
  • Construction materials testing – nationally accredited laboratory LAS-XD 1398
  • Quality inspection, load testing of bridges and roads, structural safety assessment
  • Construction supervision consultancy
  • Transport planning, preparation and verification of investment projects
  • Scientific research, technology transfer and professional training

Vision & mission

Vision: to become a leading engineering consultancy in southern Vietnam in testing, inspection and technical consulting for transport works.

Mission: to deliver reliable engineering services grounded in university knowledge – upholding integrity and accuracy in every test result and every recommendation.

People & organization

Our team includes associate professors, PhDs, masters and experienced field engineers of the UTC Campus in Ho Chi Minh City, supported by modern laboratory and field-testing equipment.

Cooperation

Domestic: departments of transport, project management units, contractors and consulting firms across southern Vietnam.

International: the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology (KICT), the International Road Assessment Programme (iRAP) and Bloomberg Philanthropies.

Quality & recognition

The company operates a quality management system aligned with national standards; the LAS-XD 1398 laboratory is periodically assessed and accredited. UTC2 JSC has received numerous certificates of merit for its contributions to major national projects.
